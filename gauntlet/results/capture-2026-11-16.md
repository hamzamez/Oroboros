# Two payloads in scope are two names: the book's wrong answer 53/76

2026-11-16. Plan item 1 of [assessment-2026-11-16](../../docs/assessment-2026-11-16.md) (hamza: "Go
ahead, always mathematical and algebraic"). No ADR: this enforces ADR 0036.

**`'just(x) -> case … { 'just(y) -> 10 * x + y }` computed 10·y + y**, 22 for "42", on every target
(the book's finding 53), and through `try` (76), since `try` is the `case` of its success arm. It now
computes 10·x + y.

## 1. The algebra, and where it failed

- `case` eliminates a sum by applying it to λ(#t, #p, …). Each clause's pattern variable is renamed
  to its slot's binder (`core/sum.go`, `renameFree`). The bodies are expanded first, so an inner
  `case` is already a λ binding the same spellings `#t` and `#p`.
- The renamed term is valid locally nameless: indices are exact, and `closeTerm` binds the outer
  `#p` to the outer λ. But it is a term **no source can write**, an outer `#p` under an inner binder
  hinted `#p`. In source text, shadowing makes such a reference impossible.
- Load's passes (module resolution `reduce.go:1471`, the state translation in `cell.go`) open a λ
  and close it again by its hints, `Fn(t.Params, f(t.Body()))`. That round trip is sound exactly
  when the hints are apart along the scope chain, which is ADR 0036's invariant. Hygiene restores
  that invariant only on the residual, after reduction. So opening both λs turned the two `#p` into
  one name, and closing the inner one captured the outer.
- **The fix is Barendregt's convention at the term's source.** A `case`'s binders are the first
  generation k (`#t-k`, `#p-k`) that none of its clause bodies mentions, as a free name or as a
  binder's hint. A `case` with no `case` inside keeps `#t` and `#p`, which is why emission is
  byte-identical.

## 2. What failed first

1. **The capture looked like the reducer's.** `bindName` opens a body with the parameter's own
   spelling without asking that it be free in the body, the locally nameless freshness condition
   x ∉ fv(t). I routed β's three bindings through `freshFor`, and the answer was still 22. User-level
   shadowing gave 42 (`fn(p) … fn(p2) …` and `fn(p) … fn(p) …`), and `case 'just(a) { … case 'just(b)
   … }` gave `10 * b + b` with no definition involved. So the term was wrong before reduction, and
   the reducer's rule is sound on source-like terms. The reducer edits were reverted, not kept as a
   rule no program reaches.
2. **A loop's match arm has the same construction**: its pattern names are renamed to the loop's
   variables by `renameFree`. Nothing in J's reader sets `Loop.Match`, so no program reaches it.
   It is recorded here, a path nothing runs, not fixed blind.

## 3. The witness

`TestTwoPayloadsInScopeAreTwoNames` (`core/capture_test.go`) checks the normal forms of four
programs:
- the chapter's nesting;
- the inner payload read first;
- three deep, every payload read at the bottom;
- the `try` chain.

Under `PlantCaseBinderCaptures` (every case at generation 0), the chapter's program computes
`10 * b + b` again.
