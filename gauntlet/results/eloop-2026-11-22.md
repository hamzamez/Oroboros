# The double word holds a loop's variable

Date: 2026-11-22. ADR 0153. hamza, 2026-11-22: "1A then 2A, go ahead always mathematical and
algebraic". This is 2A: E for a loop variable.

## The law

The residue map r : ℤ → ℤ/2¹²⁸ is injective on E = (−2¹²⁶, 2¹²⁶) and commutes with a join's choice,
r(if c then a else b) = if c then r(a) else r(b). So a loop's variable, whose value is its initial
argument's or an `again`'s, and a loop's result, a break's, is carried as its residue where every
input is a word or held in E. E's selection is the greatest fixed point over operations and joins.

A yield into ℤ of a value E can hold is rewritten by Euclid's identity in mixed radix 2³²,

    v = ⌊v/2⁶⁴⌋·2⁶⁴ + (⌊v/2³²⌋ mod 2³²)·2³² + (v mod 2³²),

each digit one of E's read-backs, so the loop keeps v in E and only the recombination at the exit is
the rung's.

## Measured first

`upto` (the sum of i below n ≤ 2⁴⁰, result `Int`) was a `*big.Int` each step on Go, its counter a
word: the decision bounds `s + i` by [0, 1 208 925 819 613 529 663 078 400] ≈ 2⁸⁰, a finite fact
inside E, and the selection excluded the join. A Collatz count, the other case the open item named,
is not A's: its counter flowing into a word result is refused (the taint), into a result declared
`Int` it is ℤ by the author's consent, and under `-checked` a checked word; it is unchanged.

## What was built

1. **The selection over joins** (ir/double.go): a loop's variables and results are candidates where
   their fact is E's and neither word's, their inputs `joinIn`, an exit's argument into a join a use
   allowed where the join is a candidate; `PlantDoubleNoJoins`.
2. **A join marked by its type**: an `int` outside both words is the double word's (`Doubles`,
   `pastBothWords`), the IR's text saying it.
3. **W14** (ir/verify.go) lets a double-word value through an exit or a loop's argument into a join
   the double word holds, and nowhere else; `PlantW14JoinsUnread`.
4. **The printers**: LLVM an i128 slot, a word stored into it extended (`asTo`); Go two `uint64`
   digits, the JVM two `long`s, set from the initial value's halves, both assigned at an `again` and a
   break, never a post-clause step; the plan's digit flows seeded at every join (`joinDigits`).
5. **`RecombineYields`** (ir/double.go), before D_A: a yield into ℤ, or into a declared set's
   ascription, of a value inside E and outside both words, rewritten where a clone's selection then
   holds it; the recombination's provenance the loop's source (`sourceOf`), `mul in s + i`;
   `PlantNoRecombine`.
6. **Tests**: `TestALoopVariableInEIsTheDoubleWords` (cmd/gen), three planted faults; the
   differential's `e-loop`, opaque, an up-sum and a down-sum returned into ℤ and a sum read back by
   `% 1000000007`, on four targets against Python.
7. **The book**: chapter 12's §12.7, a sum bounded past the word carried in two words; chapter 13's
   `no-long`, a table's sum, its text made true again.

## What failed first

- **W14** refused the loop variable at its `again`: the verifier's rule was ADR 0103's, an
  intermediate never at an exit.
- **The printers**: LLVM stored the i128 sum in an i64 slot; Go declared the variable `*big.Int = 0`
  and never computed its `again` argument, the plan's digit flows passing no join.
- **The recombination's check read the rewritten value**: the clone's yield held `w`, not `v`, and
  nothing was ever rewritten.
- **`-words`**: `TestADoubleWordNeverLeavesItsTerm` wants a product returned as `Int` refused with
  nothing in E; under `-words` the recombination is refused at the exit as its value is, so it is not
  made.
- **Imports leaking between compiles**: `cmd/gen`'s `run` did not reset the import sets, as
  `cmd/build`'s does; a test's earlier refused compile left `fmt` and `math/big` in a later Go file,
  which would not compile. Found by this step's test asserting no `math/big`; fixed.
