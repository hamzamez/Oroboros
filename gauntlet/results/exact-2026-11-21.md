# Every integer the integer it is: a set is a claim, and ρ alone chooses

Date: 2026-11-21. ADR 0150. sets-are-claims-research §3, the design A. hamza, 2026-11-21: "2. A an
intermediate outside E yes 3. report is enough, a flag (-words is just a bonus option for the
compiler)".

## The law

ρ(v) is the cheapest representation whose domain holds v's proven fact, over the chain S, U, E, ℤ. A
declared set is a claim and chooses nothing. So an operation is never refused for its size; what is
left to refuse is a value that must enter a bounded set, a sink. The demand is

    D_A = { v | v a language operation's result, fact(v) ⊄ S, fact(v) ⊄ U,
            v ∉ E's selection, v not tainted }

E's selection is the double word's greatest fixed point, computed on a clone. **The taint** is the
least fixed point, backward, of the rung's own closure: holding v exactly makes every operation over
v exact, so v may enter ℤ only if no chain of operations and joins from v reaches a sink. Its flow is
the rung's:

- a sink is a use other than the language's arithmetic, bit operation or comparison, a host's
  parameter above the word or `decimal`, a join (an `if`'s result, a loop's variable or result, a π,
  the same value renamed), or a result declared ℤ;
- a tainted result taints its operands, and a tainted receiver its inputs;
- except that a remainder taints only its divisor, an exact dividend's remainder by a word being a word
  (`big%-small`), and a bit operation whose result lies in S taints nothing, being read back.

A tainted value stays a word: refused for its size, or trapped under `-checked`, where it leaves the
word, as before. D_A is empty for every program the decision proves in a word or E, so **no program
accepted before changes**.

## What was built

1. **`ir/exact.go`**: D_A, the taint, the report (`f: N operation(s) past every word and the double
   word, held exactly: a * b; a * b * c`, where the program declared nothing above the word; the rung's
   own count where it did), and `WordsOnly`, the `-words` option of `gen` and `build`.
2. **The pipeline** demands D_A before the rung and, where the program asked for nothing, gives the rung
   the target's own plan: the host's integer of any size, or K(ℕ\*).
3. **Tests**: `TestAnIntermediatePastEIsTheIntegersOwn` (cmd/gen): a·b·c % 7 on five targets, a
   `*big.Int` on Go; `PlantExactDemandOff`, `-words` and `PlantTaintThroughRem` refuse it; a product
   stored into a table refused for its size, and trapped under `-checked`, `PlantTaintIgnored` holding
   it exactly and the store refusing it. Eleven witnesses of the words' and E's own rules run under
   `-words`, the semantics they test; the table element's witness (`PlantElementTop`) moved to
   `t[0] / 2`, where the element's fact is read.

## What failed first

- **A demanded a value an untyped parameter fed**, `shift-div`'s and `limb-subdiv`'s `run(n)` under
  -checked: the rung refused to widen n, ⊤. I relaxed the widening for "a word by its definition",
  which then accepted the constant 2⁶³ − 1 on JavaScript, where a literal past 2⁵³ loses precision;
  restricted to parameters, host results, elements and lengths it was right and reached by no program
  once the taint existed. Removed: the taint was the fix.
- **The taint missed a π**: `at(q, k + 1)`'s index reads the guard's π of `k + 1`, propagated in one
  pass after the fixed point. Inside it, `limb-subdiv` is its old program again, and its windows
  compile, 14× the baseline for a moment, is back.
- **The taint through a remainder**: `a * b * c % 7` returned as a word was refused, the result's
  sink running back through `%`; the rung's closure makes a remainder exact only through its divisor.
- **The report on every program that already asked**: a second line beside the rung's count, on
  dozens of programs whose code did not change; printed now only where A decided.
- **`-checked` tests**: a product stored into a table, trapped before, was demanded in ℤ and refused at
  the store, before the taint.
