# The gauntlet, re-run

2026-09-07. The one fixed commitment, benchmarked for the first time since
[rebench-2026-08-27](rebench-2026-08-27.md) — eleven days and thirteen results
ago, which [assessment-2026-09-06 §3.4](../../docs/assessment-2026-09-06.md)
named as process debt.

It was run now for a specific reason rather than as hygiene: **the bounds-check
fix in [jsonfmt-2026-09-07](jsonfmt-2026-09-07.md) changed the emitted code of
six programs, and two of them are gauntlet programs 1 and 2.** Verifying that the
`IsInBounds` count was unchanged is not the same as verifying parity, and this
project's rule is that a performance claim is measured.

> **All seven programs are at parity on all three targets.** Nineteen
> comparisons; the largest gap is **1.13×** and the largest win is **0.91×**,
> both on the JSON tokeniser and on different hosts.

## 4. Two process findings, and one of them is a method error I made

### 4a. Three programs are still benchmarked on the retired portable layer

> **CORRECTED 2026-09-08** — [freq-2026-09-08](freq-2026-09-08.md) §9. **It was
> ONE program, not three.** `search` has `BenchmarkSearchNative` over
> `NativeFindFirst` and `wordcount` has `BenchmarkG3WordTallyNative` over
> `NativeTally`; both were already there and this section did not look. Only
> `report` had no native benchmark, and it now does — **1,717 ns against a
> hand-written 1,731, 0.99x**. The rest of this section is right about WHY it
> survived: the benchmark had to be rewritten, because the native source exports
> `main`, which takes nothing and builds its own data.

`generated_report.go`, `generated_search.go` and `generated_wordcount.go` are
**portable-go** output — `int64` counters, `fold-range` shape. That is the debt
[assessment-2026-08-19](../../docs/assessment-2026-08-19.md) named and
[assessment-2026-09-06](../../docs/assessment-2026-09-06.md) scored as *"done for
the programs that matter"*, which was generous: it is done for dot, centroid,
generic, smooth, the tokeniser and the tree, and not for these three.

It is not a one-line fix, which is why it has survived: the native sources export
**different names and arities**. `examples/native/wordcount-go.oro` exports
`tally` and `tally-inc` where the benchmark wants one `GenWordcount`;
`report-go.oro` produces `GenMain`; `search-go.oro`'s counter is an `int` where
the test compares against `int64`. Each needs the benchmark changed, not the
generator.

**The G5 report number above is therefore a portable-layer number** and is
labelled as such. G4 is not — `G4WordCountNative` calls `NativeTallyInc` from
`gen_wordcount_native.go`, which is current.

### 4b. I nearly recorded a wrong measurement, and it is the fourth of its kind

Finding §4a meant discovering that `generated_dot.go` was portable output. I
regenerated it and five others from the native sources — and then took G1, G2 and
G7 numbers **with those files replaced**, so `BenchmarkG1GenDot`, which exists to
measure the portable layer, was measuring native code.

The numbers were plausible (0.999× for G1) and would have been recorded under the
wrong label. What caught it was noticing that `gen_dot_native.go` already existed,
which meant the regeneration was redundant and therefore wrong. Everything was
restored from backup, `git status` confirmed clean, and §1 was measured again.

**Every measurement in §1–§3 above was taken on the committed tree**, verified
clean before and after. This is the fourth benchmark-method error recorded here —
after the closure in the JS tokeniser reference, the fixed iteration count in
`bigreuse`, and process composition in `json-tree-bench` — and it is a new
species: *not a bad harness, but a measurement of a silently modified input.*

## 5. What this settles

**The commitment holds.** Seven programs, three targets, parity — including the
two programs whose emitted code changed today, which is what the run was for.

**And the emitter change is verified twice over.** `dot` and `centroid` are 0.97×
and 0.99× on Go after the narrowing was rewritten to use a fresh name, and the
`IsInBounds` count is 2 before and 2 after at the same lines. The bounds-check
elimination the narrowing exists for is intact.

**What it does not settle** is §4a: three programs are measured on a layer the
project retired, and the reason is that their benchmarks were written against a
different interface. That is a half-day of test surgery and it should be done
before the next assessment scores it as complete.
