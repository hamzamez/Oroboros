# logic3 — the logic's stage 3: a table's properties, and a host's quantified promise, 2026-11-20

hamza, 2026-11-20: "Go ahead, always mathematical and algebraic". ADR 0145's stage 3, the array
property fragment, with its programs: a table's maximum, binary search on a sorted table, and
GetWindowTextA's C string by the host's promise (L5).

## The programs

- **`examples/logic/sorted.oro`: a sorted table's ends are its least and its greatest.**
  `where ∀ i, j ∈ 0..len(a)-1 : i ≤ j ⇒ a[i] ≤ a[j]` (two nested `∀`), `ensure ∀ k : r ≤ a[k]` of
  `a[0]`, and the greatest of `a[len(a)−1]`. Prints **1 8** on every target; the call with
  `{1, 5, 3, 8}` is refused, its sortedness decided on the literal.
- **`examples/logic/maximum.oro`: a table's maximum**, its loop's invariant `∀ j ∈ 0..i-1 : a[j] ≤
  m` stated and proven by Hoare's rule. Prints **9**; the step that keeps the smaller is refused, the
  invariant not kept.
- **`examples/logic/bsearch.oro`: binary search** (Bentley's), `ensure (r ≥ 0 ⇒ r < len(a) && a[r]
  == t) && (r < 0 ⇒ ∀ k : a[k] != t)`, from sortedness and an invariant with a `∀` on each side of
  the part still searched, `∀ k ∈ 0..lo-1 : a[k] < t` and `∀ k ∈ hi..len(a)-1 : t < a[k]`. Prints
  **3 0 5 -1** on every target, and on windows the definition is kept as a function (ADR 0113).
- **`examples/logic/windowtext.oro`: a window's text is a C string, by its host's promise.**
  GetWindowTextA's `ensure 0 ≤ r ≤ len(b) − 1 && (∀ i ∈ 0..r-1 : b[i] != 0) && (r > 0 ⇒ b[r] ==
  0)`, its sentence kept as evidence; strnlen in the language, `cstrlen`, proven equal to r from it.
  Prints **1 12 12 TinyRetroPad** on both windows triples.
- **`examples/logic/segment.oro`: the promise over a segment** `t[8:40]` is over t shifted by 8: the
  NUL at `t[8 + n]` proven, at `t[n]` refused. Prints **12** on both triples.
- **The book**: §16.7's claims about a table (the unsorted call and the broken maximum refused
  before the working forms), §16.8's induction row (the adjacent form of sortedness refused), and
  §18.8, text from Windows (an arbitrary table refused at `cstrlen`'s call, then the window's text).

## What failed first

- **The disjunctive normal form exploded.** Nine instances of the sortedness schema, each an
  implication, multiplied past the budget and answered "unknown". Replaced by the search with
  propagation; the formula simplified before it, and a disjunct that is a conjunction of literals
  tested whole.
- **Facts with constants near 2⁶³ overflowed** the Omega test's checked arithmetic. A linear fact
  with a constant past ±2⁴⁰ is not given to the logic (a hypothesis dropped is sound), and a
  translation that overflows drops that hypothesis alone, the translator's scope restored.
- **A let-bound literal table, and a read at a constant index**, reached the logic as unknowns: a
  literal's read is a case split over its elements, at a constant index the element.
- **`#forall` reached the build's normal-form check** ("not in normal form"): `Residual` skips the
  logic's formers, which ε erases.
- **Binary search failed four ways in turn**: a[mid]'s index was not in the index set (the guards
  kept); a conjunction's exit lost its guards (every guard kept); the IR's intervals, which read no
  invariant, could not bound the word (the midpoint a loop variable, and the invariant's negations in
  the first clause); and an index 0 ≤ m was the linear facts' alone (the invariant's quantifier-free
  conjuncts assumed, and an index's obligation falls back to the logic where formulas are in scope).
- **Binary search compiled in 2 minutes 15 seconds.** The profile put 78% of it in the Omega test's
  search for contradictory parallel rows, quadratic in the rows, which Fourier–Motzkin's
  combinations multiply. A memo of sequents and of conjunctions bought nothing measurable, and a
  first rewrite of the search made it worse (timed out at 6 minutes 40); Pugh's parallel-row step,
  hashed, made it **2 seconds**. The memos were removed, with the fault that switched them off.
- **On windows the kept binary search failed twice.** Its `where`'s translation overflowed (the
  word's ±2⁶³; now "not proven" per hypothesis), and then its IR did not verify: **a defect older
  than this step**. An export's or kept definition's `where` is lowered to an assumption, and when
  lowering fails the statements and the value count are rolled back. But an `if`'s guard had
  renamed the binder's frame entry to a π, and the failure skipped the undo, so the body read
  `len(a)` as the length of a value number the rollback had freed and the body reused. Here W2 and
  W6 caught it; with an integer in that slot, in scope, no rule of the verifier would have. Every `where` with `&&` or `||`
  whose other side lowering does not know reached it; the frames are now restored whole, and a
  formula is never lowered at all.
- **GetWindowTextA, in order**: the target reader refused a quantifier's λ in a host `ensure` (the
  term tree takes a quantifier's binder now); a `where` holding a formula kept its arithmetic
  conjuncts from the linear facts (a split, later found unneeded once every consumer falls back to
  the logic, and removed with its fault); cstrlen's invariant was decided by Houdini, which has no
  congruence; `b[r] == 0` reached neither the linear facts nor the logic (the whole `where` to the
  logic); the host's promise was about the buffer before the call; a buffer lent to a host in a
  scope ending in host statements is refused (R1, owed since ccall); and **the scope's product
  carries none of the host's promise** (the book's finding 105), so the program uses it inside the
  scope.
- **The evidence check found a soundness defect**: the derived segment variant (ADR 0132) rewrote
  `len(b)` to `hi − lo` and left the reads `b(i)` unshifted, so a promise about the segment's i-th
  byte was stated about the table's. Latent until now, since no `where` or `ensure` read a segment's
  elements; fixed by the restriction's homomorphism, its fault planted.
- **Two planted faults that could not be**: switching off the memos and the propagation changed no
  verdict (binary search 2.6 s with or without the memos; 4.5 s without propagation, 2 s with). A
  fault is for a rule a program's verdict rests on; these are measured instead, and removed.
- **The heredoc trap**, three more times; every script with a backslash went into a file by the
  Write tool.
