# Algorithm D, and the move to K(ℕ*); M3 measured (R3c, ADR 0099)

Date: 2026-11-10. R3c of the plan, after R3b ([natnum-2026-11-10](natnum-2026-11-10.md)). Decision:
[ADR 0099](../../docs/decisions/0099-an-unbounded-integer-is-a-numeral-of-any-length-with-its-signed-size-last.md);
spec: [integers.md §1c](../../docs/spec/integers.md).

## What it is

ℤ is a Euclidean domain, and `/` and `%` are in Σ_L. A representation is a model of the integers only
if d commutes with every operation, so K(ℕ\*) without division was not yet ℤ's. On windows a quotient
by a big value was refused on both rungs.

**Long division is a Mealy machine** over the dividend's digits from the top:
- its state is the partial remainder r ∈ [0, v);
- reading digit d, the window is W = r·B + d;
- the output is q = ⌊W / v⌋, and the next state W − q·v.

It is the Euclidean step folded over the digits. **Knuth's Algorithm D** (TAOCP 2 §4.3.1) computes
the output from an estimate:
- normalize v by f = 2^s so its top digit is ≥ B/2;
- q̂ = min(⌊(W_n·B + W_{n−1}) / v_{n−1}⌋, B − 1) then satisfies q̂ − 2 ≤ q ≤ q̂ (**Theorem B**);
- D3's test, q̂·v_{n−2} > r̂·B + W_{n−2}, removes every q̂ two too large when q̂ is not capped;
- so each step adds v back at most twice, and almost never.

Every intermediate is below B² = 2⁶⁴, so all of it is U₆₄'s. A one-digit divisor is the same machine
(v_{n−2} = W_{n−2} = 0, so q̂ is exact).

**Which rung.** D was written once, on K(ℕ\*), the general representation:
- V_n's carrier lies inside K(ℕ\*)'s, and both are injective into ℤ;
- so a bounded program the fixed limbs cannot finish **moves to K(ℕ\*) whole**, as on Go it moves to
  `math/big`;
- its one set is enforced there by ADR 0029's two observables, the sign and the bit length, read off
  the header and the top digit (`fit`, `fit_signed`).

Whether V_n should exist at all was ADR 0099 item 5's measurement, below: it should.

## What was built

- **`emit/natnum.oro`, Algorithm D**: `div` and `rem`, with their parts:
  - `qhat` (D3);
  - `msub` (D4, in B^{n+1}'s complement with the sign in entry n + 1);
  - `addv` and `fix1` (D6, at most twice);
  - `nfactor` (D1), `topcmp` and `slice` (the machine's first state);
  - `unnorm` (D8).

  The quotient's size is decided first from u's top window against v, so its descending build
  writes the header first, at the counter.
- **The bound on K(ℕ\*)**: `fit` and `fit_signed`, `big-fit`'s two forms.
- **`ir.LowerLimbs`**: `big/` and `big%` by a numeral are `div` and `rem` on K(ℕ\*), and a `big-fit`
  there is its check.
- **`ir.SelectRung`**: on a target with no bignum, an operation V_n lacks moves the program to
  K(ℕ\*) with its one set, in place of the refusal.
- **`emit.Target.HoldsZ`**: the capability "this target can hold ℤ" was read as "it has a host
  bignum" in two places in the checker (`check.go`). Both now read the declaration: a host bignum, or
  `repr big limbs`. A definition declaring `Nat` was refused on windows by that stale premise.
- **`big-nat-div`**, a differential case of sixteen inputs:
  - the four sign combinations, quotient and remainder;
  - two pairs that take the add-back;
  - a quotient of 0, an exact quotient and its neighbour;
  - a two-digit divisor, the least D takes.

## Witnesses

- **A model of the library's algorithm**, step for step in Python, against `divmod` on 200,000 inputs
  weighted to extreme digits (0, 1, 2³¹ − 1, 2³¹, B − 2, B − 1): every quotient and remainder
  agreed. Theorem B held at every step, every intermediate was below 2⁶⁴, and 2,804 steps took the
  add-back. The case's add-back inputs come from it.
- **`TestAQuotientByANumeralIsAlgorithmDs`** (`cmd/build`), on windows: two add-back inputs print
  Python's numerals. **The planted fault** `emit.PlantNoAddBack` drops D6, and they differ.
- **`TestABoundedQuotientMovesToTheNumeralsOfAnyLength`** (`cmd/build`), on windows: a program in
  0..2³⁰⁰ dividing by a big value prints Python's quotients, then crashes with the bound's reason on
  a value past 2³⁰⁰. **The planted fault** `ir.PlantNoNatFallback` keeps it on the fixed limbs, where
  it is refused.
- `TestTheNumeralsOfAnyLengthCheckAsATheory` covers `div`, `rem`, `fit` and `fit-signed`.
- `big-nat-div`, `big-nat-ops` and `big-fib` agree on go, js, java and windows.

## What was measured

**M3** (integers-research §7), best of five, two rounds, serially. The same source is compiled for
windows (K(ℕ\*)) and for Go (`math/big`), and the outputs are identical, digest for digest:

| program | windows, K(ℕ\*) | Go, `math/big` | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| fact(20,000), a word product per step | 394.0 / 390.1 ms | 34.9 / 34.5 ms | 11.3× |
| 2,000 quotients, 4,000-bit by 2,250-bit (D) | 265.6 / 264.3 ms | 84.4 / 84.8 ms | 3.1× |
| fib(10⁵), additions | 252.6 / 251.8 ms | 28.7 / 27.9 ms | 8.9× |

**ADR 0099 item 5**: is K(ℕ\*) as fast as V_n on a bounded set? 10⁷ additions of 10²¹, the R1 loop,
declared `0..2^200` (V₉) and `Nat` (K(ℕ\*)):

| | round 1 | round 2 |
|---|---|---|
| V₉ | 136.9 ms | 136.0 ms |
| K(ℕ\*) | 601.3 ms | 597.1 ms |

**V_n stays**, 4.4× faster where it applies. On K(ℕ\*) each sum is a heap table of run-time size,
and R1b's tokens are static, so none is reused. That is **R1d's trigger**, "a measurement naming
one", now met: a data stack, or a token of run-time size.

**Where M3's factors come from** is not measured one cause at a time here; the candidates:
- `math/big` has 64-bit limbs and assembly carry chains, so half the digits;
- every K(ℕ\*) digit read is masked (`dig` states the representation's invariant as its mask);
- every result is a fresh heap table.

The quotient loop is the closest (3.1×): D's inner work is per digit pair, and `math/big` divides
by the same algorithm, so the digit width and the allocation are what differ.

**The check**:
- `examples/big/`'s `fact`, `fib`, `pair` and `power` now emit on windows. Each declares `Nat`, and
  each was refused by the stale capability premise.
- Driven through `big_str`, their functions print the same 513 bytes on windows and on Go: fact(30),
  fib(1000), pair(12) and 997⁶⁴.
- Their compile times are flagged against the baseline's refusals (15 ms): `pair` 500 ms and `power`
  921 ms, paired, the cost of the inlined instances (deliberately not next: an instance emitted once).
- The counts went from 5,656 of 5,710 operations and 638 of 662 loops to 6,070 of 6,124 and 662 of
  686, every added one proven; the unproven 54 and 24 are unchanged.
- Two tests pinned the refusals R3c removes, and are restated with their premises computed:
  - `TestATargetWithoutABignumRefusesAndSaysSo`: windows holds ℤ, and the same target with its limb
    rung removed refuses;
  - `TestAnUnsupportedLimbOperationIsRefusedByName`: windows moves `a / b` and `a % b` to K(ℕ\*), and
    with the fallback planted off the fixed limbs refuse each by name.

## What failed first

The non-relational intervals cannot hold D's classic invariants, and each failure was restated, not
clamped:
- **r̂ = top − q̂·v_t** needs q̂ ≤ top / v_t for r̂ ≥ 0. r̂ = top mod v_t in the uncapped branch has
  the interval [0, v_t) by itself, and D3 runs only there, unrolled twice.
- **The short division's digit cur / k** needs r < k. It is not needed: n = 1 is the same machine.
- **D8's digit** r_i / f + (r_{i+1} mod f)·(B / f) needs r mod f < f against B / f. Its definition
  ⌊(r_{i+1}·B + r_i) / f⌋ mod B is bounded by itself.
- **A guard's quotient recomputed in its arm**: with no CSE, the guard's π lands on the first copy.
  It is bound once before the case.
- **`nfactor`'s doubling** is no additive step for size-change termination. The shift count s ≤ 31
  is the measure, and f keeps its own guard for its interval.
- **A one-digit divisor's zero** sat behind a negated conjunction. The case is nested, and k ≥ 1 is
  the arm's own path fact.

One guard is a total extension, not a clamp, and is stated as one: `qhat` answers 0 for a top digit
of 0, outside the theory's domain (v is normalized). The theory cannot state a precondition on a
table's contents. The same holds for `dig`'s mask.

**And the checker said windows cannot hold ℤ** (`HoldsZ`, above), found by M3's bounded comparison.

## What was not built

- **M4, B = 2⁶⁴ over i128**: it needs LLVM's native iN (ADR 0083's deferred widths), a build, not a
  measurement.
- **R1d**: its trigger is met by the 4.4× above.
- **D on the fixed limbs**: not needed, since the move to K(ℕ\*) supplies it at the cost of the
  program's run.
- **The masked reads replaced by a relational invariant** (a digit is in [0, B) by the table's
  element range): the same debt as the fixed limbs' `at`.
