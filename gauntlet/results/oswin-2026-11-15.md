# `os` on windows: the portable operating system over Win32, written in J

Date: 2026-11-15. [ADR 0112](../../docs/decisions/0112-os-on-windows-is-a-library-over-kernel32-and-valid-utf-8-is-recognized-by-its-monoid.md),
[oswin-research.md](../../docs/oswin-research.md). hamza: "go ahead with os on windows, always
mathematical and algebraic", and V2 for the UTF-8 wall. **`read-loop` and `text-of` run on windows**,
equal to Go, JavaScript, the JVM and their expect lines: no differential case skips windows but the two
`u128` ones, which test a Go-only library. Every `os/host` name runs there, each in a program of its own.
`roundtrip.oro`, which calls all nine in one `main`, waits on ADR 0102's P8 (below).

## The algebra

- **A Win32 failure is a term**: r(b, e) = err e if b is the function's sentinel, else ok b, with
  GetLastError right after its call by the effect discipline. No niche, and errors.md §4.4's `fails`
  form is not built. `error` is the DWORDs.
- **An out-parameter is a cell** (ADR 0048): f : X × T\* → R, keeping no address, is X × T → R × T. On
  LLVM the cell is an `alloca` of T's C type, and in a prototype `cell(T)` is `T*`.
- **A borrow that also fills cells** is B × X × T → B × T × R: the lent buffers' versions, then the
  cells' values, then the result.
- **V, the valid UTF-8, is recognizable**: V = φ⁻¹(F) for φ into Table 3-7's transition monoid.
  `utf8_valid` is decided by φ on a literal and by a guard otherwise, and `utf8_text` is ι_V.
- **text_of = ι_V ∘ r** on windows, r the maximal-subpart repair, a Mealy machine in J.
- **Args = d* ∘ parse ∘ GetCommandLineA**, with Go-on-Windows' parse (`readNextArg`), a counter automaton.

## What was built

- `lib/os/windows.oro`, `os/host` in J over kernel32: ReadFile, WriteFile, ReadAt, the kinds'
  predicates (Go's `Errno.Is`), text_of, Args, Getenv. Each name builds its sum once, at its tail.
- `lib/io/windows.oro`: print_line, print, print_int over `win/print` and WriteFile.
- `targets/windows/kernel32.oro`: CreateFileA, GetFileSizeEx, SetFilePointerEx, CloseHandle,
  ReadFile and WriteFileC (templates, a buffer at an offset, the count a cell), GetCommandLineA,
  lstrlenA, lstrcpynA, GetEnvironmentVariableA; `DWORD` and `LARGE_INTEGER` as sets.
- `targets/windows/windows.oro`: `set error = 0..2^32-1`; `realize utf8_text`.
- `targets/llvm`: `set cell(A) = host "ptr"`; `llvm.address` (`ptrtoint`); `lang.v_accepts`, the
  automaton in J, realizing `utf8_valid`.
- Σ_L: `utf8_valid`, `utf8_text`, realized on Go (`utf8.Valid`, `string(b)`), JavaScript (`fatal`
  TextDecoder; `ignoreBOM`, so a leading U+FEFF stays), the JVM (`REPORT` decoder) and windows.
  strings.md §11, inventory.md.
- `ir/llvm`: cells (`cell-fresh`, `cell-ref`, `cell-get`); the C ABI's `cell(T)` and byte tables.
- Witnesses with planted faults:
  - `TestValidUTF8IsRecognizedByItsMonoid` (`PlantUTF8Fault`: a literal read as valid without φ);
  - `TestAnOutParameterIsACell` (`PlantCellUnread`: the size comes back 0);
  - `TestTheRepairsGuardCatchesAWrongRepair` (`PlantFragment` rewrites the repair to write U+FFFD
    as two bytes, and the guard's abandon is reached).

## What failed first, and what each fix is

The cell is the first J library a target provides, and the first borrowing definition to answer a
product. So most of what failed were the compiler's untried paths:

- **A cell call with one result** (`core/outcell.go`) was eliminated as a product, `#elim`: every Go
  cell call (the Scan family) answers a product. One result is a let.
- **The value read back from a cell** had the bare word's fact. It now has the cell's element set,
  the C type's range (`ir/interval.go`).
- **Prototype sets were checked before manifest sets unfolded**, and `mapTypeNames` did not descend
  into `cell`. The check moved after the unfolding.
- **A product with a sum among its factors** (ReadAt's `(n, r)`) failed in four places:
  - the header reader (`TypeTerm`) refused the product;
  - `ProdTypes` split an applied factor's own commas (now at depth zero);
  - a sum factor was not flattened into its tag and slots;
  - the claim check counted no lent buffer (`lentSig`).
- **A program header named a target's manifest set** (`error`) unresolved. It is now unfolded
  (`unfoldSig`, `UnfoldType`, and `unboundSet` through aliases).
- **A borrow that also fills cells** met two translations. `translateBorrow` now does both.
- **A borrowing definition answering a product** handed back (b′, (n, e)), a tuple nested in a
  tuple. A name holds one value at the IR, so it can't be bound and taken apart. The fix is in the
  tails: `tailArity` counts the product, and `spliceTails` rewrites each tail (v, x…, p…) to
  ((p…, v₁ … vₙ), x…) in place (`core/cell.go`, `place.go`). The lowering flattens a factor that is
  several values, A × (B × C) ≅ A × B × C. **Only a definition whose header declares a product
  hands its factors back flat**, as a host call declaring n results does. Flattening every
  borrowing definition reached `lib/os.ReadAt` on every host: its sum became a top-level component,
  and Go's `roundtrip` grew from 174 to 250 lines by case-of-case, the same answers. Gated on the
  header, Go's text is the baseline's.
- **An η-reduced rule `(table m b)`, and a constant rule `(table n 0)`**, were not lowered under
  `alloc`. The first is the prefix of b; the second yields the constant.
- **The LLVM template `%r0`**, a write-borrow's result after its buffer, is `%r` at arity one.
- **A conjunction of an atom and a linear fact**, `nulfree(k) && n <= len(b)`, was outside the
  decided fragment. Γ ⊢ a ∧ b iff each conjunct holds, each by its own route.
- **The reducer's growth**:
  - case-of-case copies a consumer into every constructor site of a sum, and every `if` of tuples
    copies its continuation, so sums built in nested branches multiplied the program past the fuel.
    Each name now builds its sum once, at its tail, and its raw steps are loops of one iteration,
    the join point (ADR 0031);
  - a join loop inside `text_of`, under `Args`' rule, made reduction diverge, and it was reverted;
  - `roundtrip` still passes `core.MaxDepth`, below.
- **Relational invariants the intervals do not keep**, each stated once where it bites:
  - a clause or clamp that is the identity on the invariant (`readinto`'s rest ≥ 1, `repair`'s
    k ≤ 3i);
  - a total store or read (`put`, `at`);
  - a domain (`repair` past (2⁴⁷ − 13)/3 bytes, `ReadAt`'s off ≤ 2⁶²).
- **Not fixed, found**:
  - `DischargeRanges` swallows a lowering error. The refinement layer then refuses alone, naming an
    obligation the IR would have proven, and that misled the diagnosis twice (a rule never allocated
    is the real error).
  - `alloc(o[a:b])` of a segment is erased though the segment flows into a host call.
- **The rule transfer's ⊥ for a body that never yields**: suspected again, re-applied, shown unneeded
  by its planted fault, and reverted (radix-2026-11-15's rule).

## Measured

- The differential suite: every case agrees on every target, `read-loop` and `text-of` on four.
- The full check passes. Seven programs now emit on windows that were refused for want of `os`:
  `wc`, `count`, `jsonfmt`, `fnv`, `crc32`, `errors/ignore`, and the two cases. `wc` and `count`
  print what Go prints on the same file. So the proof counts rise to 7,462 of 7,516 operations
  (the 54 left unchanged) and 826 of 856 loops (six more left: the cell's read and write loops,
  whose progress is the count the host returns). Go's, JavaScript's and the JVM's text is
  unchanged.
- The compile-time gate's nine "slower" windows compiles are refusals become compiles, 15–46 ms of
  failing to find `os` against 0.3–11 s of compiling. `freq` takes 50 s to reach a refusal: the
  repair's obligation on a byte read from a host-filled table, which waits with `roundtrip`'s scale.
- Not measured: speed. The file programs on windows are the next step, against hand-written code.

## What was not built

- **`roundtrip.oro`, `ledger.oro`, `pe.oro` and `freq.oro` on windows.** Its nine calls inline nine J definitions into one `main`, and
  reduction recurses past `core.MaxDepth` (100,000 frames). The same calls in a flat sequence, not
  nested in cases, build with 3× the fuel, in 19 s. The algebra's answer is ADR 0102's P8, a headed definition kept as a
  function decided once under its parameters' sets, as K(ℕ\*)'s instances are. This program is its
  trigger, so `TestEveryPortableNameRunsOnEveryHost` skips windows, saying so.
- **The structural check of a J cell**: `emit/portable_test.go` compares the host cells' primitives,
  and a J cell's names are definitions.
- **The decision proving the repair's image in V** (V3), and errors.md §4.4's `fails` form.
