# P8, step 1: a program's definitions kept as functions on windows

Date: 2026-11-15. [ADR 0113](../../docs/decisions/0113-a-programs-definitions-are-kept-as-functions-where-the-target-prints-them.md),
[p8-research.md](../../docs/p8-research.md), ADR 0102's P8. hamza: "go with your recommendations,
always mathematical and algebraic" (Q1–Q4). The ledger was registered before building (p8-research
§5), and every figure below is measured against the K = ∅ build of the same commit (`ORO_KEEP_NONE=1`).

**`roundtrip`, `ledger` and `freq` now emit on windows.** With K = ∅, `roundtrip` and `ledger` pass
the reducer's step limit, and `freq` is refused on an obligation the inlined residual cannot prove.
With their definitions kept, each builds in about 3 s (`freq` in 26 s). No program that built before
is refused. Go's, JavaScript's and the JVM's emitted text is byte-identical.

## The algebra

- **Keeping a definition is an extension by definitions** (Theorem 1), so no answer changes. Kept or
  inlined, a call means the same thing; only the proofs, the size and the time differ.
- **Depth**: inlined, a sequence of calls nests each continuation at the previous body's tails, so
  depths add. Kept, they take a maximum (Proposition A).
- **Case-of-case**: inlined, a sum's consumer is copied into each constructor site (Proposition B).
  Kept, it is written once.
- **A lent buffer is B × X → B × R written in place** (ADR 0070):
  - the B handed back is the identity on the argument;
  - so it is the argument's own storage, checked by the greatest fixed point of "v is p, or every
    source of v is" (`sameStorage`), over joins, loops, πs and stores;
  - so its length is the argument's, len(b′) = len(b), a theorem of the arrow and not an `ensure`;
  - and the call is never pure, since it writes b.
- **A result's type is a claim decided at the definition** (ADR 0072):
  - a stated result is met with the hull its body proves;
  - an unstated one *is* the hull, or, with no bounded hull, its representation's codomain
    (Proposition D).
- **A verdict is monotone in K, not invariant** (corrects ADR 0113 item 1). Under K = ∅ an
  obligation is decided on the inlined residual; under K it is decided once under the contract, and
  at each call by the arguments' sets.
  - Theorem 3 says the contract can prove less, and P4's net catches every such case.
  - The refinement layer's incompleteness on a long residual makes the converse possible too: the
    contract proves what the inlined copy lost. That is `freq`.
  - So the property that holds is: every program accepted with K = ∅ is accepted with K, with the
    same answer. A program refused with K = ∅ may be accepted with K, and then by a proof.

## What was built

- **The choice** (`ir/keep.go`, `chooseKept`): K is computed per program and target, and the source
  names nothing. A member is:
  - a dynamic definition, first-order, with every parameter's set stated;
  - its `where` no more than its parameters' range premises (`core.WhereIsRanges`: the reader
    desugars `k ∈ 0..100` into one, and that is the contract itself);
  - paying: |f| · max(π(f) − 1, 1) ≥ T, with T = 64.
  - The call graph's π undercounts the reducer's copies, which case-of-case makes. So a definition
    called once pays when its body reaches T, and LLVM's inliner takes back what is cheaper inlined
    (P7).
- **Reduction** (`core/reduce.go`): a kept name is not δ-unfolded. It is an operation symbol to the
  join checks (`isMultiApp`, `selectTails`).
- **The instance** (`buildKept`): the body is normalized once, its obligations are decided under its
  header, and it takes the entry's pipeline (`Entry`, `Refusal`, `SizeRefusal`, `CheckBornIn`). Then:
  - the result hulls (`resultElems`, `resultHull`) are set;
  - the function is registered as a program function (`registerProgramFunc`; several results are an
    aggregate);
  - a definition that lends buffers gets its threaded view (`registerThreaded`). Its function is
    `f~lent`: `~` is an identifier character of IR_P's text that no J name holds.
- **Purity** (`funcPure`): a call is pure iff its body calls nothing impure and it lends no buffer.
- **P4, at three levels**:
  1. a definition its contract does not prove is dropped from K (`KeepDrop`);
  2. a call outside the contract drops its callee (`KeptCallee`);
  3. **the net**: a program refused with K ≠ ∅ is built again with K = ∅, and that verdict is the
     program's.
- **The drivers** (`cmd/build`, `cmd/gen`):
  - the keep loop, with a note for each definition dropped and for the net;
  - each kept definition's proofs reported once per program (`ir.KeptCounts`), so `cmd/check`'s
    counts keep them;
  - `cmd/build` decides `Refusal` and `SizeRefusal` inside the loop, so a refusal K caused is retried.
- **The refinement layer** (`emit/refine.go`): an in-place component's length is its argument's,
  whatever the call's precondition. This closes `roundtrip`'s `buf[0]` after `os.ReadAt`, which was
  refused with K = ∅ as well.
- **`lib/os/windows.oro`**: `repair`'s bound was off by one (found under its contract); `readfile`'s
  prefix length is clamped where the boundary forgets `m ≤ len(b)` (a cost, below).

### Witnesses, each with its planted fault

| witness | rule | planted fault |
|---|---|---|
| `TestASequenceOfKeptCallsTakesTheMaximumDepth` | six `os` calls on windows print Go's output | `PlantKeepNothing` (K = ∅): the step limit; `PlantTypingResult`: refused or wrong |
| `TestALentBufferIsWrittenByItsCall` | after a lending call, the caller knows the buffer's declared elements only | `PlantLentPure`: the stale zero fill proves `show`'s argument, and the program calls `show` outside its set (9223372036854775955) |
| `TestAWriteBorrowKeepsItsLength` | `buf[3]` after `os.ReadAt` into four bytes, `f` on Go and windows | `PlantInPlaceNoLength`: the index refused |
| `TestALentBufferIsHandedBackAsItsOwnStorage` (IR) | a loop of stores is the buffer's storage; a join with a second buffer is not | `PlantNotInPlace`: the join accepted |

`PlantTypingResult` is the bug that shipped inside this step. Windows' `open` returns a 0 that
unification typed `failed`'s 1..2³²−1. A caller read that type as the call's fact, found its success
arm empty, typed `readfile`'s result `Array(0..0)`, and refused `text_of`'s argument. **An IR type is
a representation, never a fact**: a callee's result is a fact only by its claim or its hull.

## What failed first

1. **The syntactic π kept almost nothing.** `put`'s copies are made by case-of-case, not by its
   name's occurrences. Hence the amended pays rule.
2. **Every ranged parameter excluded its definition.** `k ∈ 0..100` desugars into a `where`. Hence
   `WhereIsRanges`.
3. **The in-place check refused a loop of stores.** `sameStorage` saw joins and loops, not a buffer's
   πs and `set`s.
4. **A kept result was weaker than its body**: `fill`'s `% 10`, typed `Int`, made the caller's sum
   unprovable. This is Theorem 3, recovered by the hull.
5. **The hull read typing's types as facts** (`PlantTypingResult`, above).
6. **A kept lending function's name**, `#host:…`, is no identifier of IR_P's text. `cmd/check`'s `ir`
   step caught it on seven windows programs.
7. **`repair`'s bound was off by one.** Inlined, the facts of each site hid it; under its contract
   it showed.

- **Compile time**: `cmd/check`'s gate flags `freq` (1.54× paired), `roundtrip` (4.35×), `ledger`
  (4.28×) and `pe` (3.31×). For the first three, the baseline is a refusal and the new time is a
  build: the cost of a program that now compiles. `pe` pays a failed keep and the net's second
  build. `jsonfmt` is 0.27× (0.53× here), `crc32` 1.48× paired, cleared by the gate. The rest of the
  corpus is 1.04× the baseline by the median.
- **Run time**: unchanged, 0.985–1.03, inside the noise floor. LLVM inlines the kept functions back
  where that pays (P7). Measured on the five programs that run both ways.
- **Code size**: within ±512 bytes (one 512-byte section) on the programs that built before.
- **Proofs**: no operation or loop became unproven on any program that built before.
  - The corpus counts are **7,293 of 7,347** operations and **762 of 800** loops, against 7,462 of
    7,516 and 826 of 856. Each kept function's proofs are counted once per program.
  - The totals fall because an operation inlined into several sites is now one operation, proven
    once. `jsonfmt`, for example, goes from 742 to 174, `count` from 312 to 123.
  - Unproven operations: 54, unchanged. Unproven loops: every program that built before keeps its
    own (1 each, the `os` cell's read loop).
  - The 8 added belong to the three programs that now emit: `freq` 5, `roundtrip` 2, `ledger` 1.
    Each is the `os` cell's read or write loop, whose progress is a count the host returns.
    30 + 8 = 38.
- **Refusals changed**: 3 programs now emit (`roundtrip`, `ledger`, `freq`). None that emitted is
  refused. Refusal text changed on `pe`, `discard`, `ignore`, `sieve-win`, `shortcircuit-win` and
  `codecs`, each by a note: a definition dropped, or the net.
- **The net's count**: 5 of the windows corpus's programs take it, each named in its outcome.
  - `discard` is meant to be refused, and is refused the same way with K and with K = ∅.
  - The other four are Theorem 3, and all at one place: a caller reads a kept result through its type
    alone, and the body's facts relating it to the arguments are not exported:
    - `sieve-win` (`c[k]`, k < len(c)) and `codecs` (`bytes.get`'s 8 + w ≤ len(d)): the result's
      length;
    - `crc32` (`tab[(crc ⊕ b[i]) & 255]`): its element set, which the type states and the refinement
      layer does not read;
    - `pe` (`bytes.get`'s bound on `ReadAt`'s count): `n ≤ len(b′)`.
  - The cure is one construction: a kept definition's derived postcondition, the facts its body
    proves of its results over its parameters (len(r) = n, n ≤ len(b′)), assumed at each call
    (ADR 0072's claim, extended from sets to formulas). Owed, with step 3's `ensure` on named
    results.
- **Emitted text changed**: on windows, 24 changes, each accepted with this reason. Go, JavaScript
  and the JVM: none (K = ∅ there by the capability).
- **Compiler lines**: `ir/keep.go` 563 new, and +470 / −159 elsewhere (`cmd/gen`, `cmd/build`,
  `ir/progfunc.go`, `core`, `emit`): about 874 non-test lines. Tests: 256 lines
  (`cmd/build/keep_test.go`, `ir/keep_test.go`).
- **Language lines**: `lib/os/windows.oro` +8 / −3: `repair`'s bound, and `readfile`'s clamp
  `m1 = min(max(m, 0), len(b))`. The clamp is a missing proof: kept, `readinto`'s count comes back
  without `n ≤ len(b)`, which inlined it had.
- **Rules made less general**: none. A rule was made *more* general: a `where` of range premises no
  longer blocks a contract.
- **Not measured, owed**: reduction depth and steps per program. K = ∅ fails at the step limit on
  `roundtrip` and `ledger`, so the ratio is unbounded there; on the programs that build both ways it
  is owed. Clang's share of the compile is also owed.

## What was not built

- **P8 step 3**:
  - Proposition D for an unannotated parameter (`print_int`, `write`, `slice` stay inlined);
  - lifting a `where` beyond range premises (Theorem 4);
  - J's `ensure` on named results, which would give `readinto` its `n ≤ len(b)` and retire
    `readfile`'s clamp and `pe`'s fallback.
- **A finer P4**: the net drops all of K. Dropping the one definition a refusal names would keep the
  rest.
- **A kept definition's derived postcondition** (`sieve-win`, `codecs`, `crc32`, `pe`).
- **K on Go, JavaScript and the JVM**: their printers do not print program functions.
- **The cell's hand-written join points**, removable now that their definitions are kept: a later
  step, with its own ledger.
