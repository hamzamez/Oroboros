# A pair only where it computes: JavaScript's unsigned word as a selection

Date: 2026-11-21. ADR 0151. hamza, 2026-11-21, on the three options for JavaScript's open refusals:
"I agree. record 2 and 3 as optimizations for another time" (option 1, the pairs as a selection, with
option 2's comparison and signed reading).

## The law

ρ(v) is the cheapest representation whose domain holds v's proven fact **and whose operations cover
v's uses**. On every target but JavaScript the second condition is the first's consequence. On
JavaScript U is two 32-bit digits (ADR 0083), whose algebra lacks a division by a non-power of two, a
variable shift and an operation whose result leaves U. A value of U leaves the pair exactly when it
meets one of them (`PairsLeaving`, ir/pairs.go), D_A demands it, and the rung's closure takes the rest:
the greatest fixed point of "every use covered" on the pair is the least on ℤ.

## Measured first

A digit sum over x ∈ [0, 2⁶⁴), 200,000 values, node 26, best of 7, two runs:

| | ns a value |
|---|---|
| BigInt throughout | 531, 535 |
| the pair, each `/ 10` and `% 10` conjugated through BigInt, r ∘ op_ℤ ∘ ι | 1,869, 1,917 |
| the pair, short division by 10 on its digits (option 2, not built) | 338, 317 |

So a value leaves whole (3.5× against conjugation), and short division, recorded for later, would
buy 1.6× on this loop (u64repr-2026-09-22 measured 4.3× on division by a small constant).

## What was built

1. **The selection** (`PairsLeaving`, `covers`): a use the pair expands is covered; one the rung
   computes and the pair does not sends the value to D_A (ir/exact.go), a loop's variable as a demand
   and not an operation in the report. A residue stays a pair, entering ℤ by ι : U ↪ ℤ, `pair-bigint`
   at the rung's `big-of`; a closed constant is never demanded (the rung spells it).
2. **Comparison**, lexicographic on (h, l), an `if` on the high digits' equality.
3. **The signed reading**: a ring or bit operation's operands by their residues mod 2⁶⁴, a result in S
   read h − 2³²·[h ≥ 2³¹], the high digit and the value ascribed their decided facts (`toWord`).
4. **A word divided by a pair above it**: |a| < b gives a / b = 0, a rem b = a (`smallByLarge`), the
   Mersenne hash's first step.
5. **ℤ leaves as U does**: `big-box`, the decimal string `pair-box` gives, at a host's parameter of any
   type; a result declared in U takes the BigInt it would have been; neither a sink for the taint.
6. **The facts the rung hides, ascribed** where a value of U left the pair for the host's integer: a
   loop's word variables, where a variable held exactly has its fact in U, and `big-word`'s and
   `big%-small`'s results over such a value, each its decided fact on ℤ (`ascribe`, `leftPair`).
   Size-change termination reads an ascription as the identity it is, the(x) = x (`plThe` in
   ir/sct.go, `deltaFrom`'s case in ir/trip.go), so a renamed variable is the same variable for its
   steps.
7. **The report**: `N operation(s) of the unsigned word that its pair does not compute, held exactly`,
   apart from those past every word.
8. **Tests**: `TestAPairLeavesForTheIntegers` (cmd/build), one answer on four targets, and eight
   planted faults: `PlantPairCompareLowOnly` and `PlantPairUnsignedReading` answer wrongly;
   `PlantPairsKeepAll`, `PlantPairWordUnascribed`, `PlantRungFactsForgotten` and
   `PlantPairSmallByLarge` refuse; `PlantPairSinks` prints otherwise. Four differential cases' JavaScript
   skips removed (`u64-digits`, `u64-square`, `u64-values`, `mersenne-hash`); `double-word`'s restated,
   a checked skip for the finding below, and the skip test's control since `u64-digits` has none.

## What failed first

- **A constant demanded**: the Mersenne hash at n = 1, `(0 + 0) % (2⁶¹ − 1)`, a word by a constant past
  JavaScript's word; the selection sent the constant to D_A, which the rung cannot hold as a variable.
  A constant is a value, spelled by its spine; and a word by a divisor above it reads no digit.
- **The ascriptions missed `big%-small`**: the digit sum's `s + v % 10` was unbounded on JavaScript
  once v was a BigInt, `v % 10` the host call's whole word; the digit sum in the test passed only
  because its parameter declared U.
- **The ascriptions on K(ℕ\*) did not end**: gated on D_A non-empty, they ran on `examples/big/stein.oro`
  for windows, gcd over `Nat`, whose D_A is non-empty and whose rung is K(ℕ\*); the compile, 40 s at
  the baseline, had not ended after an hour (2,100 s of CPU), stopping the first full check in its
  emission step. Gated on "A chose the plan" instead, the digit sum over a declared U was refused
  again. The line is the representation: on the host's integer a value held exactly is ⊤ to the
  decision taken again, which the ascription answers; on K(ℕ\*) they are not placed, and stein's
  emitted code is the baseline's, byte for byte, on four targets. Why they do not end there is not
  measured; owed if a program on K(ℕ\*) meets the lost bound.
- **The ascriptions cost termination proofs**: gated on D_A non-empty and the host's integer, they
  ran on `popcount`, `render` and the `render` case on Go, the JVM and JavaScript, whose `Nat` loops
  declare the rung themselves; each lost a loop's proof, SCT reading `p = the(q)` as no step of q,
  while `stein` gained three, the totals equal by chance. Gated on a value of U left the pair, and the
  ascription read as the identity, none is lost and `stein` on JavaScript proves two more.
- **ADR 0150's width, and two wrong answers trying to fix it**: `x * 3 / 4` under a declared S₆₄ on
  JavaScript (S₆₄ above its word) stops at run time, "the declared range is too small", an intermediate
  D_A holds checked against the program's one set (ADR 0029), a run-time check by bit length placed at
  every operation on the rung. Shipped in ADR 0150, reached by `double-word` once the pairs compared.
  First fix, the width raised to every proven fact past the word: `big-sign`'s yield −2²⁰¹, past its
  declared −2²⁰⁰..2²⁰⁰, printed on every host where it must stop, and `big-negmid`'s limbs plan was made
  signed, which its conversion has no form for. Second fix, no fit at an operation of D_A unless an
  export's result claims it (its yields followed back through joins): `big-sign` printed again, its
  `calc` inlined into the harness's `main`, where nothing marks a definition's result as claimed. One
  width is two things, the representation's room and the declared sets' enforcement, and after inlining
  a claim above the word cannot be told from an intermediate. **Reverted to HEAD's rule; the finding,
  owed**: a claim above the word carried through inlining as a mark, the one set enforced where a set
  claims. `double-word` skips JavaScript for it, a checked skip, and the test pins the stop.
- **`big-box` unregistered**: a name the rung writes must be in the target loader's list of the
  rung's operations (emit/target.go), or the target's declaration is not read.
- **My test's table literal past 2⁵³**: an element is a word by storage; the value is made by
  arithmetic instead.
