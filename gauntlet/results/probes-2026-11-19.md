None is refused. Either the shapes mapvalue met differ from these, or a step since has proven them.

## 2. `PlantLoopThresholdFault` (llvm-render-2026-11-07)

The plant widens every loop up to the function's thresholds instead of its own.
`TestALimbValueRendersOnWindows` records that it no longer reaches its program. The failure it was
written for needs a loop whose lower end descends one threshold per round, through more of the
function's constants than 16 rounds pass, and a use after the loop that depends on that end. The
probe had 39 loops counting to 25, 50, …, 975, every value used, and a countdown from n ≤ 1000
whose exit k indexes `t[0 - k]`. It is proven on Go and windows with the plant on and off, and
prints 19507 both times.

The countdown's exit is bounded by its guard's negation and the refinement layer, whatever the
widening reaches, as `ascii`'s length is (strings-2026-11-16). Per-loop thresholds are precision,
and every threshold set is sound. The plant stays a witness owed: a program whose proof needs a
loop's own thresholds, which neither this construction nor the corpus has.
