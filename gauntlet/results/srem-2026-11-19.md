# srem — a remainder by a constant of a proven natural is the signed instruction (ADR 0138), 2026-11-19

ADR 0138 item 2: measured on the corpus first, every constant divisor's site both ways.

## The measurement

**The sites.** With every constant, the rule changed 728 instructions in 31 windows programs (774 in
34 on windows32), 301 of `big-signed-ops`' 310 alone a division by 2²⁴, the fixed limbs' base. With
the corpus's exception for a power of two (below), it changes **54 instructions in 20 programs on
each triple**, each a `urem`/`udiv` by a constant becoming `srem`/`sdiv`: the programs' own `% 10`,
`% 7`, `% 1000003`, and the digits' divisions by 10ᵏ. No other line of any program changed.

**Three builds of each program**: HEAD's `urem`/`udiv` (0); `srem`/`sdiv` wherever the divisor is
proven a constant (1); the same only where the constant is not a power of two (2), since `urem` by
2^k is one `and`, and `srem` by it, with a dividend LLVM does not know non-negative, is a sign fixup.
The builds came from a measurement compiler: a scratch copy of HEAD whose realization choice reads
`ORO_SREMK`. Each program ran 7 rounds, the three variants interleaved and their order rotated
each round. The machine was quiet, and the outputs agreed in every round.

The loop is phi-range §4's 1.42× against C, now at C's 319 ms. **The limbs' factorial decides the
rule.** Its base is 2²⁴, so its digits are `% 2^24` and `/ 2^24`. There `urem` is one `and` and
`udiv` one `lshr`, and `srem`/`sdiv` add a sign fixup that LLVM keeps, since it cannot see the
dividend non-negative: 1.13×. Excluding powers of two (variant 2) is 1.003 there and keeps the
loop's gain. The derivation predicted exactly this before the measurement: on ℕ ∩ S₆₄ the four
instructions are one function, and the cost model differs by the divisor's shape. Every other
program is within 0.96–1.03, inside the noise floor.

This was found only because the accepted diff was read. 301 of `big-signed-ops`' 310 changed sites
divided by 2²⁴, and no timed program of the first corpus ran on fixed limbs. A corpus measurement
is only as good as its coverage of the sites that change.

And the measurement found a wrong answer: 20000! printed as 20 zeros (print-2026-11-19).

## What was built

ADR 0138 item 1, as data in the target, with the corpus's exception:
- `targets/llvm/instructions.oro` declares `sdiv_natk` and `srem_natk` on ℕ ∩ S₆₄,
  `where point(b) && !pow2(b)`;
- `llvm.oro`'s `realize` lines list them before `udiv_nat` and `urem_nat`.

`point(x)` and `pow2(x)` are a realization's `where` predicates, decided on the facts by
`ir/realize.go`'s `whereHolds`: x's proven set is one value, and that value is 2ᵏ. Of two realizations as narrow, `narrowest` takes the
first listed. A variable divisor keeps ADR 0088's unsigned instructions, and a dividend not
proven natural keeps the first realization, `lang.rem`'s `srem`.

`TestARemainderByAConstantIsSigned` (`ir/realize_test.go`): `x / 7 + x % 7` on ℕ takes the
signed pair, and `x / y + x % y` and `x / 8 + x % 8` the unsigned pair. The fault `ir.PlantPointUndecided` leaves the
constant on the unsigned pair.
