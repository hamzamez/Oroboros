# tier2 — windows32 as the second tier of `cmd/check`, 2026-11-19

hamza, 2026-11-19, on the check's time against its coverage: "a full check should include
everything, but [we] can make windows32 a second tier platform, check less often than the
others". Decided-list item 4.

## What was built

`go run ./cmd/check -tier2` runs every step of the check, and two steps more:
- **`emission-windows32`**: the emission sweep on windows32 alone. Every `.oro` under `examples/`,
  `lib/` and the differential cases is compared with windows32's own baseline,
  `gauntlet/check/windows32/` (its `outcomes.txt`, `compiletime.txt`, `testdata/emitted/` and
  `ACCEPTED.md`), under the same rules and its own compile-time gate. It runs alone, after
  `emission`, since the gate measures CPU time.
- **`differential-windows32`**: `run.go -targets windows32`, every case built, run and checked
  against its `// expect:` on windows32. A case names windows32 in its `// skip:` and `// checked:`
  lines as it names any target. A skip naming windows32 is a checked claim there. When it is the
  only target run, it is evaluated against the expectation.

`-accept` with `-tier2` keeps both baselines, each needing its own differential to pass in the run.
The tier runs at each assessment, and before accepting a change to the LLVM layer, `lib/rt` or
windows32's targets (gauntlet/check/README.md).

The code: `cmd/check`'s checker gained a baseline directory and a tier, with
`emissionStep`/`differentialStep` naming the steps its `-accept` reads. The sweep's targets are
swapped for the alone step (`withTargets`). `run.go` gained `-targets` and `allTargets`, and
windows32's driver and artifact entries, which are windows'.

## What failed first

- **`map-keys`** traps under `-checked` on windows32, as on windows: `acc·100 + k` widens, and the
  map's counts sum cells of a table whose elements are unbounded. Its `// checked:` line names
  windows32 now, with windows' reason.
- **u128's two cases** skip windows32 as they skip windows, for the reason they state. The
  rewrite in the language (item 5) removes every one of their skips.
